Our story

Sweet moments, made with cariño

Prima is a little café on wheels, bringing the flavors of home to wherever you are.

Our drinks start with Mexican classics like café de olla, horchata, tres leches, churros and mazapán, then get poured into specialty coffee and creamy matcha. Every cup is made by hand, one at a time, the way you'd make it for family.
